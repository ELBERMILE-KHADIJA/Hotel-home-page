About HOTEL AGADIR

HOTEL AGADIR is a haven of peace and luxury, nestled in the heart of Morocco's Atlas Mountains. Our eco-friendly retreat offers breathtaking views, traditional Moroccan architecture, and world-class amenities for an unforgettable experience.

Luxury Accommodations

Spacious suites and villas with panoramic views.

Wellness & Spa

Rejuvenate with signature treatments and yoga sessions.

Gourmet Dining

Savor Moroccan and international cuisine.

Our Rooms

Luxury Suite

Elegant suites with private terraces and mountain views.

Private Villa

Exclusive villas for ultimate privacy and comfort.

Spa & Wellness

Indulge in our holistic spa treatments, inspired by ancient Moroccan rituals. Enjoy hammams, massages, and yoga in a serene environment.

Dining

Experience culinary excellence with fresh, local ingredients. From traditional tagines to international flavors, our chefs create memorable meals.

Activities

Explore the Atlas Mountains with guided hikes, horse riding, or simply relax by the infinity pool.
